    How the score works
    • Length is how many characters you typed.
    • Entropy is a measure of how unpredictable the password is. We take the size of the character pool you used (26 for lowercase, 26 for uppercase, 10 for digits, 32 if you used a symbol from this set: ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) , . ? " : { } | < >) and multiply the length by log2 of that pool.
    • Score adds up three things: points for the mix of characters (10 for lowercase, 10 for uppercase, 10 for a digit, 20 for a symbol), 3 points for every character, and half the entropy rounded down. It stops at 100.
    • Common passwords (123456, password, 123456789, 12345678, 12345, admin, welcome) always score 0, whatever the capitalisation.
    • Verdict follows the score: under 40 is Weak, under 60 Moderate, under 80 Strong, and 80 or more Very Strong.
    • Good to know. The common list is short and only catches the whole password, and the score doesn't look for patterns like 1234 or dictionary words. Blocking passwords you've used before isn't part of this prototype.
